# Creative Review

Use for a structured review of a proposed image, video concept, or copy draft.

## Inputs

Use the supplied audience, offer, product facts, objective, channel, brand rules, assets, and evidence. If an asset cannot be inspected, say so. Do not claim to have watched a video from a thumbnail or transcript alone.

## Review

1. State the intended audience and the behavior the creative should encourage.
2. Check the opening, product clarity, offer, proof, and call to action.
3. Verify factual and numerical claims against the supplied evidence.
4. Identify specific issues and proposed revisions. Preserve approved wording unless changes are requested.
5. Separate observable properties from subjective judgments and performance hypotheses.
6. Propose one testable change at a time, with the comparison and success measure defined in advance.
7. Flag what needs human review before launch.

## Output

- Brief alignment
- Observed strengths
- Issues requiring correction
- Proposed revisions
- Test hypothesis and measurement
- Evidence gaps and approval point

Do not promise conversion lift, infer audience traits from appearance, fabricate testimonials, or treat an aesthetic preference as proven performance. Prepare recommendations only; do not publish, launch ads, or change account settings.
