Have useful drafts ready for the morning

My role: Designed a reusable AI skill for planning and preparing my work. Its instructions are private.

Start with the work that matters soon

I built a day-ahead planning skill around my assigned tasks and recent messages. It focuses on work due soon and new items that need attention, rather than letting an old overdue list bury everything.

It sorts work into what is already covered, what is blocked, what can be drafted and what needs my decision.

Prepare the work before asking me to act

The skill is designed to research and draft the items it can prepare. It also looks for people waiting on a response. The output is one brief that is easy to read on a phone.

I can approve selected items by their number. External email stays in draft form for me to send. The skill's project documentation describes use in Claude Cowork and Claude Code.

Why I designed it this way

A list of tasks still leaves me with all the preparation. I want AI to do useful work ahead of time so my attention goes to review and decisions.

The skill keeps that decision with me. Its scope is my own work and connected sources; it is not permission for an assistant to act everywhere.

Current evidence

The reusable skill and setup documentation exist. This portfolio review inspected the artifact rather than running the overnight routine or sending messages. I do not claim a measured time saving or a current live schedule.

The project shows how I turn a recurring personal need into clear instructions that an AI assistant can follow.
