# Fictional decision brief: should a campaign receive more budget?

**All figures, entities, and circumstances in this example are fictional. This is a workflow example, not a portfolio outcome.**

## Decision needed

Should Example Brand A receive a budget increase this week?

## Observed evidence

The supplied fictional dataset describes three complete days:

- Advertising spend: $1,200.
- Platform-attributed revenue: $3,360.
- Recent platform ROAS: $3,360 / $1,200 = 2.8x.
- Prior comparison ROAS: 3.2x.
- Relative ROAS change: (2.8 / 3.2 - 1) × 100 = -12.5%.
- Delivery is reported as budget-constrained.

## Missing evidence

No independent store-revenue reconciliation or contribution-margin target was supplied. Attribution maturity and the persistence of the constraint are also unknown.

## Recommendation

Keep the current budget while checking revenue reconciliation, attribution maturity, and the performance target. A delivery constraint establishes an opportunity to spend more; it does not establish that more spending would be worthwhile.

## What would change the recommendation

Consider a bounded increase if independent revenue supports the recent result, performance meets the business target, and the constraint persists across additional complete-day observations.

## Approval and verification

Any budget change requires the decision owner's approval. After an approved change, verify the actual budget and track efficiency against the agreed comparison. Do not report the recommendation as an executed change.
