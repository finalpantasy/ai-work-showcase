Building systems a marketing team can use

My role: Paid-media leadership, hiring, training and process design. The examples below describe my work without sharing employee or client records.

Turn experience into a repeatable way of working

I have led and developed 5+ paid-media strategists and specialists. Part of that job is making good work easier to repeat when accounts grow or the team changes.

I built systems for onboarding, account ownership, audits, campaign checks, reporting and launch review. My career records also describe a 200+ page audit and optimization guide.

The goal is practical: someone should know what to check, what good looks like and what needs attention next.

Make creative testing easier to learn from

My creative testing work covers the idea being tested, the audience, opening message, format, offer, landing page and signs that an ad is wearing out.

I have also designed a creative operating system covering strategy, production, checks, launch and learning. Its discovery work is complete, with leadership decisions still needed before rollout. I keep that stage clear rather than presenting the plan as a finished launch.

Help the team learn AI

I am developing an AI training program with a path from beginner through advanced work. The current approach curates and sequences existing instruction, with prerequisites and completion requirements.

The program is meant to make learning manageable for people with real work to do. It has a shared curriculum home and an earlier prototype kept as reference material. The prototype is separate from the replacement curriculum.

Keep busy periods organized

I created a seasonal planning structure that connects account work to shared deadlines, readiness checks, review points and post-event decisions.

I also build account coverage notes around budgets, targets, timing, owners and risks. That gives another person enough context to keep the work moving.

Hire for useful judgment

My hiring work includes technical screens, interview scorecards and strategist evaluation criteria. I look for account diagnosis, clear client communication, creative thinking and the ability to work independently.

What this work shows

I build both the tools and the way people use them. The evidence supports the guides, frameworks and planning work. Consistent team adoption takes follow-through, which remains a focus of my work.
