# Turning creative ideas into clearer tests

My role: Creative strategy, testing standards and AI-assisted workflow design. Internal templates and client work remain private.

## The problem

A team can produce many ads without learning much. If the message, audience, offer and format all change at once, it is hard to know what worked.

I build creative workflows that start with the question we want to answer. What is stopping this customer from buying? What proof would help? What message are we missing?

## My work

My career evidence describes repeatable testing workflows covering hooks, audiences, customer objections, formats, copy, products, landing pages and creative fatigue.

The paid-media platform connects that thinking to a message audit and a brief generator. A gap can become a draft brief, with two message options and a clear test variable.

The tool uses available brand information, customer feedback, prior work and current advertising. It flags missing proof so a person can check it before the brief reaches design.

## Make the handoff useful

A designer needs more than “make another ad.” The brief should say who it is for, what the message needs to change, what proof is allowed and what the person should do next.

My work translates those decisions into a format the team can use. The brief can be copied or downloaded for the next step in the workflow.

## Keep learning after launch

I have designed a broader creative operating system covering production, review, launch, testing and iteration. That project is at the planning stage, with leadership decisions still pending before rollout.

I distinguish that plan from the existing platform tools and testing practices. The business aim is the same: help the team make purposeful creative and learn from it.

## What I am good at

I connect customer thinking, ad performance and the practical details of getting work made. AI helps with research and drafting. I set the direction and review whether the result answers the original question.
