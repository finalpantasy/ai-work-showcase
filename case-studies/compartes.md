# Compartés: award-winning holiday growth

My role: Helped lead the paid-media team and owned the award submission. I used AI to support creative ideas, copy, analysis and tooling. These are team campaign results.

## The business goal

The job was to grow holiday sales while keeping advertising efficient. That required creative, budgets and measurement to work together during a busy sales period.

The 2025 holiday campaign received the 2026 Meta Agency Award for Performance Campaign of the Year by a Boutique Agency in North America, as reported in the published campaign coverage.

## The campaign results

The published case reports these year-over-year results for the nine-day Black Friday and Cyber Monday period:

| Measure | Change |
|---|---|
| Meta advertising spend | +99% |
| Purchases attributed to Meta | +120.7% |
| Revenue returned per dollar of Meta spend | +2.9% |
| Cost per purchase | −9.8% |

The eight-week holiday season is a separate window. Shopify recorded $2.84 million in total sales, up 33.1%, and first-time customer orders rose 42.1%. Those are store-wide results.

## Where I used AI

I used AI regularly to help work through creative ideas, copy, research and analysis. I also worked on tools that support account decisions and reduce repeated preparation.

My contribution was to direct that work toward the campaign's needs, review the output and help the team turn useful ideas into action. AI supported the work; the reported results do not isolate an AI-only effect.
