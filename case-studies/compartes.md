# Compartés: AI-assisted holiday campaign strategy

**Context:** 2025 holiday campaign, recognized at the 2026 Meta Agency Awards.

## Problem

Grow holiday sales while maintaining advertising efficiency as investment increases.

## My contribution

I helped lead the team behind the campaign. My contribution combined performance marketing strategy with AI-assisted creative and copy development, analysis, and workflow tooling. AI supported preparation and iteration; people retained responsibility for creative decisions, budget choices, and campaign execution.

## Approach

The team coordinated creative variety, budget guardrails, and recurring performance reviews. Decisions considered both platform-attributed performance and total-store results.

## Publicly reported campaign results

For the nine-day Black Friday and Cyber Monday period, compared with the prior year:

| Measure | Year-over-year change |
| --- | --- |
| Meta advertising spend | Increased 99% |
| Meta-attributed purchases | Increased 120.7% |
| Meta ROAS | Improved 2.9% |
| Cost per purchase | Decreased 9.8% |

These are relative changes, not percentage-point differences. The campaign received Performance Campaign of the Year by a Boutique Agency at the 2026 Meta Agency Awards.

## What the results establish

The campaign increased attributed purchase volume while improving reported efficiency. These are team outcomes. They do not isolate AI's incremental effect or establish that one contributor caused the result. Platform-attributed purchases are not the same measure as total-store sales.

## Learning

Creative preparation, measurement, and a clear decision rhythm help a team respond to peak demand. AI can support research and iteration while people remain accountable for the commercial decisions.
