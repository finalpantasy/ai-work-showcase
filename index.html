      How I direct AI work

      Commercial context comes first. Models support delivery; they do not establish that a result is correct.

      1. Define the decision

        Who needs to act, what outcome matters, and which evidence is available?

      2. Bound the work

        Specify ownership, permitted actions, failure cases and acceptance criteria.

      3. Build and review

        Use AI-assisted implementation, inspect the changes and test the behavior.

      4. Verify the result

        Check observed state, preserve receipts and separate tests from user acceptance.
