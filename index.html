Compartés was my client. Its 2025 holiday campaign won the 2026 Meta Agency Award for Performance Campaign of the Year by a Boutique Agency in North America. It is also the best example of how I run an account: agree the target first, spend in steps, and judge the result against the plan.

I helped lead the elk team on the campaign. I built the $200,000 five-stage Meta plan, set the weekly budget steps, decided what creative we needed and wrote the award submission. Creators, designers and strategists made the ads and ran the account day to day.

01

Agree the floor before spending

We set a Meta return floor of 4x and split $200,000 into five stages that followed the prior year’s demand. Budgets rose 10 to 20% a week, and only after the last step held.

02

Push hardest where the numbers hold

Over the nine days around Black Friday, Meta spend nearly doubled, purchases more than doubled and cost per purchase fell 10%.

03

Take the trade you planned for

Full-season return slipped from about 5.1x to 4.6x while store sales grew 33% and first-time orders grew 42%. Growing the business inside the floor was the goal, not the highest possible ratio.

02 / The team

The standard behind it.

That campaign was not one person’s instinct. It came from a written standard my whole team works from, and from reviewing work against it every week.

200+

pages
Audit, optimization & training guide

I joined elk in 2017, ran paid media as Director from 2020 and became VP of Paid Media in April 2026. Six strategists report to me. I hire through panel interviews, test projects and scorecards, and brought on two strategists this year.

The standard is written down: the audit and optimization guide, the Ad Training Library of playbooks and exercises, a launch checklist with screenshot proof and same-day checks, and a report format that puts the answer first. Biweekly 1:1s, monthly account audits and a weekly paid-media roundtable are where it gets taught.

When the team started using AI, the rules stayed the same: plan the work, get a person’s approval, check the result against real account numbers. Writing that down was the easy part. Getting six busy people to follow it on every account, every day, is why I built AdNexus.

03 / AdNexus

AdNexus

The standard,
built into software.

AdNexus is elk’s AI operating system for paid media. It connects Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, Microsoft Advertising, GA4, Triple Whale, Slack, Asana and meeting notes, and runs every account through the checks my team learns by hand. A strategist asks a question in plain English and gets a sourced answer, a dashboard or a change ready for approval.

165 tools
18 scheduled monitors
34 client workspaces
How I built it

I wrote the requirements from years of reviewing the team’s work, then built it myself with Claude Code and Codex on Next.js, TypeScript and Postgres. Between July 15 and October 12, 2026, 860 of my pull requests were merged through CI on a protected main branch.

The four rules are in the code. AdNexus reads the client’s goals before it recommends anything. Every change to Google or Meta is previewed, approved, re-checked against the live account, read back and logged with a way to undo it. New campaigns and ads start paused.

What it does

Less time collecting facts.
More time deciding.

Google Ads

Find the waste first.

Eighteen monitors run on a schedule, covering search terms, ad copy, landing pages, stock, seasonal messaging and budget pacing. They stay quiet while an account is healthy. When something breaks, the strategist gets the finding, the evidence and a fix ready to approve.

Meta Ads

Decide what to test next.

Creative Confidence scores each ad statistically, so a new ad is not written off after one day of spend. Notes are written for whoever reads them next: an explanation for the client, the next concept for the designer.

The whole account

Keep the history with the numbers.

Platform results sit next to the Slack threads, Asana tasks, meeting notes and playbook guidance for the same client. When results move, the What Changed investigator compares the two periods and says where the change is and what to check next.
