Google Ads

Find what’s wasting budget.

AdNexus helps review search terms, budgets, campaign results and conversion tracking. It can suggest negative keywords, draft ad copy using the client’s offer, and prepare Performance Max campaigns for review.

That cuts down the manual checks and setup, leaving more time to decide what to fix or test.

Meta Ads

Learn which ads to test next.

AdNexus helps us compare ad results, review creative, write briefs and prepare new ads for upload. Client feedback stays with the work, and proposed account changes are reviewed before they go live.

We can use what we learn from one round of ads to prepare the next.

The whole account

Keep the client’s history close.

AdNexus brings Google and Meta results together with GA4, Triple Whale, client conversations, project notes and the Ad Training Library. Teams can see what changed, what we’ve tried and what we still need to check.

People can understand the account without hunting through separate reports and conversations.

02 / How I work

How I decide
what to change.

Before I change a campaign, I want to know its goal, how the business makes money and what the client has asked us to do.

01

Finding new customers

I want to know whether these customers buy again, what the business earns from each sale, and how long it takes purchases to show up in reporting.

02

Helping customers buy more

An ad might be there to introduce a second product or increase the value of an order. I check whether it’s doing that job and whether the extra sales are worth the cost.

03

Testing a new idea

I check when the test started, what we’re comparing it with, and what the client agreed to. I weigh the early results against the time and budget we set aside to learn.

AdNexus brings account data and client history together, so we can work through these questions without starting from scratch.

B / SummonerDesktop AI workspaceIn development

Built around how I work

Keep the work together.

I use several AI tools to build products and work through problems. Switching between conversations makes it easy to lose track of what changed, what we decided and where a project left off.

I’m developing Summoner, a desktop AI harness that brings projects, conversations, tasks and results into one workspace. I set the product direction and build it with AI assistance on an open-source foundation.

I want to keep the history of the work, choose which model and account I’m using, get a second opinion when I need one, and review changes before keeping them. The prototype is designed around those needs.

Current statusWorking prototype

The screens and controls are working. Live AI account connections are still being integrated.

  1. 01

    Pick up where you left off

    Keep a project’s conversations, tasks, drafts and results together.

  2. 02

    See where information came from

    See source links and spot information that is old or missing.

  3. 03

    Get a second opinion

    Choose a piece of work for another model to review, and keep the feedback beside it.

  4. 04

    Review and undo changes

    Compare changes and restore earlier versions before approving work.

What works today

The prototype demonstrates project and account choices, information views, model review and code review with sample data. Its model controls don’t yet run live AI requests.

The information views show sources and flag old or missing material. Access is limited to the selected project and source account. Connections to private knowledge libraries are planned.

You choose the account and when to ask for a review. If the selected account isn’t available, the prototype stops. Live account connections and code review on real projects aren’t ready for everyday use.
